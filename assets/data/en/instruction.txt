RAPID DOMINANCE — HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE
Destroy every enemy team's townhalls while protecting your own. A player is eliminated after losing their final townhall.

TURNS AND RESOURCES
Every action uses action points (AP). You receive 8 AP per round, plus bonuses for controlled land and additional townhalls. Every 8 experience points raises your AP limit by one. Mines generate gold; mines built on gold deposits are much more productive. Barracks immediately recruit troops, which are spent when attacking.

ATTACKING AND CAPTURING
Select a sector and press A, click Attack, or right-click it. The target must share an edge with your territory and touch at least two of your sectors (corners count for the second sector). A sector next to your townhall only needs the shared edge. Buildings and walls must be destroyed before their sector can be captured.

An empty sector surrounded on all four edges by your territory is annexed automatically. This costs no additional AP or troops.

BUILDINGS
Townhall (T): protects you from elimination and increases AP income.
Mine (M): generates gold every round; deposits yield the most.
Barrack (B): immediately recruits troops; existing barracks improve recruitment.
Observation tower (O): expands fog-of-war vision; a damaged tower has less range.
Wooden wall (W) / Stone wall (S): defensive structures with increasing resistance.

Repair a damaged building with R. Demolish one of your buildings with D; your final townhall cannot be demolished. In team games, select an ally's sector and press G to send 50 gold.

FOG OF WAR
Your territory and nearby sectors are visible. Previously explored sectors remain dimly visible. Observation towers reveal a wider area.

CONTROLS
Left click: select sector
Right click / A: attack selected sector
Arrow keys: move selection
Space / F: end turn
L: abandon sector (costs 1 AP)
T / M / B / O / W / S: build
R: repair building
D: demolish building
G: transfer 50 gold to the selected ally

The action panel shows building costs and the selected sector's owner, type and resistance.
